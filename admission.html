Admission Process

Thank you for considering Little Angels Modern High School for your child's education. We are committed to providing a nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence and personal growth.

Admissions for the academic year 2025-26 are now open for all grades from Nursery to Class XII. We follow a holistic admission process that evaluates students based on their academic potential, interests, and character.

State-of-the-art infrastructure and facilities

Experienced and dedicated faculty

Comprehensive curriculum focusing on holistic development

Excellent track record in academics and co-curricular activities

Safe and secure learning environment

Please fill out the preliminary application form, and our admission team will get in touch with you for the next steps.

Admission Process

1

Application Submission

Fill out and submit the online application form with all required details.

2

Document Verification

Submit necessary documents for verification by our admission team.

3

Entrance Assessment

Students applying for Grade 1 and above may need to take an assessment test.

4

Personal Interview

A brief interaction with the student and parents to understand expectations.

5

Admission Confirmation

Selected candidates will receive an admission offer letter.
